Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

1. Introduction +

Hapin, Inc. operates a service to help you discover films, find showtimes at theaters near you, and set a reminder for when a film opens. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use and share it, and your choices and rights.

2. Information We Collect +

Personal information you provide to us. You can use Hapin without an account. If you choose to sign in with Apple or Google, we collect:

  • Email address, or an Apple-generated private relay address
  • Name, if Apple or Google provides it
  • An account identifier from Apple or Google

Whether or not you sign in, we receive the film, ZIP code and date you search for showtimes.

If you sign in with Apple and choose to hide your email, we receive an Apple-generated private relay address. We don't share relay addresses with third parties, consistent with Apple's requirements.

If you sign in with Google, we receive only your name, email address and Google account identifier. We don't access your calendar, contacts or any other Google Account data.

When you tap Reserve, the theater's own ticketing page opens in your browser. The theater or its ticketing provider collects payment and any other purchase details directly from you, under its own privacy policy. Hapin doesn't receive or store your payment information.

Information automatically collected. We automatically collect certain technical information to provide the service and keep it working:

  • Log data, such as IP address and timestamps, when the app requests films and showtimes from our servers
  • Crash and diagnostic data, such as device model, operating system version and app version, when the app crashes or hits an error. If you're signed in, this is linked to your account identifier.
  • Approximate location, inferred from your IP address, to show nearby theaters and showtimes

Reminders. When you tap "Notify me when it's out," Hapin schedules the reminder on your device. The reminder stays on your device and isn't sent to us.

3. How We Use Your Information +

We use your information to:

  • Provide and operate the services, including showing showtimes near you, scheduling reminders on your device, and directing you to theaters' ticketing pages
  • Communicate with you, including responding to support inquiries
  • Improve and maintain the services, including troubleshooting crashes and keeping them secure
  • If you allow tracking, share your email address and approximate location with advertising platforms so movie ads can be shown to you and to people with similar interests (see "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" below)
  • Prevent fraud and abuse
  • Comply with the law

We do not sell your personal information for money. If you allow tracking, we may share limited information with advertising platforms, which may be considered "selling" or "sharing" under California and other state privacy laws. See "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" below for details and how to opt out.

4. How We Share Your Information +

Service providers. We share information with vendors bound by contract to use it only for the services they provide us: data hosting, sign-in and crash reporting. These include Google, through Firebase (hosting, sign-in and Crashlytics crash reporting), and Apple and Google for Sign in with Apple and Sign in with Google. We estimate your location from your IP address using a database on our own servers (MaxMind GeoLite); your IP address isn't sent to MaxMind.

Advertising platforms. See "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" below.

Ticketing and exhibitor partners. See "Ticketing and Exhibitor Partners" below.

Studios and exhibitors. We don't currently share your personal information with studios or exhibitors.

Professional advisors. We may disclose information to lawyers, auditors and accountants, under confidentiality obligations, as needed for their services to us.

Legal and safety. We may disclose information where required by law or necessary to protect the rights, property or safety of Hapin, our users or others.

Business transfers. In a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, personal information may transfer as part of the deal. We'll notify you if this subjects your information to a different policy.

Aggregated and de-identified data. We may share information that no longer reasonably identifies you; this Policy doesn't restrict that use.

5. Ticketing and Exhibitor Partners +

When you tap Reserve, Hapin opens the theater's own ticketing page in your browser. Hapin doesn't send the theater your name, email or account details, and doesn't sell tickets itself.

Your purchase, including payment, is handled by the theater or its ticketing provider under its own privacy policy, and we encourage you to review it before purchasing.

This section will be updated as our ticketing and promotional partnerships expand.

6. Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information +

Hapin doesn't sell your personal information for money.

Advertising platforms and lookalike audiences. When you first open Hapin, your device asks whether to allow Hapin to track you. If you allow it, we may provide advertising platforms with identifiers derived from your contact information (such as a hashed version of your email address) and your approximate location. These platforms may use this to show movie ads to you and to people with similar interests, including "lookalike" audiences: groups of new people who share characteristics with Hapin's users. Under the CCPA and similar state laws, this may be considered a "sale" or "sharing" of personal information, even though we don't receive money for it. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, we don't share your information this way.

You can opt out anytime by turning off tracking for Hapin in your iPhone's Settings (Privacy & Security > Tracking), or by contacting support@hapin.co, and we'll honor your request.

Opting out doesn't affect your ability to use Hapin or receive reminders.

7. Sensitive Personal Information +

Precise geolocation is classified as Sensitive Personal Information under the CCPA/CPRA and similar laws, with additional protections.

If you tap "Use my current location," Hapin asks for your device's location permission and finds your ZIP code on your device using Apple's location services. Only the ZIP code is sent to our servers; we don't receive or store your precise location. We:

  • Use your location only with your device-level permission
  • Use it solely to show theaters and showtimes near you
  • Don't use it for profiling or cross-context advertising
  • Let you turn it off anytime in your device settings, and you can always enter a ZIP code instead

California residents can limit the use of Sensitive Personal Information to necessary purposes. Contact support@hapin.co with questions.

8. Data Retention +

We retain information as long as necessary to provide the services and meet legal obligations, then delete or de-identify it:

  • Account information (name, email address and account identifier) is kept until you delete your account. You can delete it anytime in the app under Settings > Delete Account.
  • Crash and diagnostic data is kept by our crash-reporting provider for a limited period.
  • Server logs, including IP addresses, are kept for a limited period for troubleshooting and security.

You can also contact support@hapin.co to delete your information; we'll delete or de-identify what we can, keeping only what's legally required.

9. Security +

We use industry-standard safeguards to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, use or disclosure. Report security issues to support@hapin.co.

10. Your Rights +

Under the CCPA and similar comprehensive privacy laws in states including Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Utah, Texas and Oregon, you have the right to:

  • Know what categories of personal information we collect, use and disclose, and why
  • Access, correct or delete your personal information (see "Data Retention" above)
  • Obtain a portable copy of your personal information
  • Opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information, including for targeted advertising (see "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" above)
  • Limit the use of Sensitive Personal Information (see above)

We won't discriminate against you for exercising these rights. Contact us at support@hapin.co, or by mail using the address in "Contact Us" below, to exercise them. We'll verify your identity where required and respond within the legal timeframe. If we deny a request, you may appeal by replying to our denial; if denied again, you may contact your state's Attorney General.

Cookies. Our website may use cookies to operate and improve the site. Control them through your browser settings; disabling some may affect functionality.

11. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) +

Hapin's services aren't directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their personal information. If we learn we've collected information from a child under 13 without verified parental consent, we'll delete it promptly.

Contact support@hapin.co if you believe we've collected a child's information. We may suspend or terminate accounts if we believe a user misrepresented their age.

12. Changes to This Policy +

We may update this policy. Changes will update the "Last Updated" date above and, where legally required, come with additional notice. Continued use after a change takes effect means you accept the update.
